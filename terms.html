What this site is

pariscomedy.com ("Paris Comedy") is a comedy-listings and community platform. It indexes English-language and French stand-up comedy shows in Paris and connects comics, bookers, and audiences.

Listings and accuracy

Listings may change. Show dates, times, lineups, and venues are subject to change without notice. Users, bookers, and comedians who submit information to the platform are responsible for the accuracy of their submissions.

We do not guarantee that every comedy show in Paris is listed on pariscomedy.com. The platform grows by user opt-in, scraping of public sources, and editorial curation; coverage is best-effort.

Third-party tickets and platforms

Many shows are ticketed through external platforms (Eventbrite, BilletRéduc, Fnac, Helloasso, the venue's own page). pariscomedy.com is not the seller and does not guarantee third-party ticket availability, refunds, or seating policies. Issues with a ticket purchase must be resolved with the platform or venue that processed the transaction.
