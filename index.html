Frequently Asked Questions
Everything you need to know
Are the shows in English?
Most shows on Paris Comedy are English-language or bilingual (English + French). Each listing is clearly labelled. If you only speak English, look for shows tagged EN — there are regular English and bilingual shows throughout the week.
How do I buy tickets?
Most shows list tickets through Eventbrite. Click "Check current listing" on any show card and confirm the date on the source before you go. Some shows are free or pay-what-you-can at the door.
How do I promote a listing?
Promoted placement is available through our paid Featured Show Promo product (the SKU name) and via editorial picks. See pricing for promo tiers (€5 / 7 days · €15 / 30 days · €40 / 90 days). Listings in the directory are not ranked by source — discovery surface is neutral.
