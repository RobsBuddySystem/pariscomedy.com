Frequently Asked Questions
Everything you need to know
Are the shows in English?
Most shows on Paris Comedy are English-language or bilingual (English + French). Each listing is clearly labelled. If you only speak English, look for shows tagged EN — there's something on every night of the week.
How do I buy tickets?
Most shows sell tickets through Eventbrite. Click "Book tickets" on any show card and you'll be taken directly to the listing. Some shows are free or pay-what-you-can at the door.
Can I book Robert for a private event?
Yes. Robert performs for corporate events, private parties, festivals, and birthdays — in Paris and internationally. Use the Book a Show form to get in touch.
How do I list my comedy show?
Listings are €1/month and paid via Lightning (Bitcoin). Go to Book / List a Show, fill in your show details, and pay directly to payments@pariscomedy.com. We'll publish within 24 hours.
