🌍 Lost in Translation

Words that comedy understands better than dictionaries do.

A bilingual hook that clicks with expats and tourists

French people don't give you information — they give you a social riddle.

That's exactly why live bilingual comedy works in Paris: the joke isn't just the words, it's the cultural confusion underneath them. If you've ever frozen during la bise, misread a deadly bien sûr, or heard on verra and still felt weirdly hopeful, this room is for you.

“On verra” = “This is already dead. Please stop talking.”
French isn't a language — it's a customer service mood ring.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does it work?
Reserve your spot on Eventbrite. We ask for one drink minimum at the bar, and at the end of the show we pass the hat for the performers. Give what you feel the laughs were worth. 🎩
Do I need to speak French?
Nope! Most shows are in English or bilingual (French & English). You'll laugh either way — comedy is universal.
Do I need to reserve?
Strongly recommended. Shows fill up and reservations get priority seating. It's free to reserve on Eventbrite — takes 30 seconds.
Can I perform at the open mic?
Absolutely! The open mic at 19:00 is open to all. Sign up at the door or use our contact form. Bring 5 minutes of material and zero shame.
Can I book a comedian for my event?
Yes! Corporate events, private parties, festivals — we'll match you with the perfect comic. Email Contact form.
Where exactly is Velvet Bar?
39 Rue de Douai, 75009 Paris — right in Pigalle. Metro: Pigalle (M2/M12). It's a basement bar with great vibes. You'll find it.
