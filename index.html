Frequently Asked Questions
Everything you need to know
Are the shows in English?
Shows on Paris Comedy are English or French. Each listing is clearly labelled. If you only speak English, look for shows tagged EN — there are regular English-language shows throughout the week.
How do I buy tickets?
Open the show's page on Paris Comedy first to check the latest venue, day, time, language, and source link. If the show uses Eventbrite or another ticket platform, the ticket button appears inside the Paris Comedy show page. Some shows are free or pay-what-you-can at the door.
How do I promote a listing?
Promoted placement is available through our paid Featured Show Promo product (the SKU name) and via editorial picks. See pricing for promo tiers (€5 / 7 days · €15 / 30 days · €40 / 90 days). Listings in the directory are not ranked by source — discovery surface is neutral.
Want to know what’s on tonight?
One weekly email — verified Paris comedy listings, new venues, new comics. No spam, unsubscribe anytime.
