Frequently Asked Questions
Everything you need to know
Are the shows in English?
Most shows on Paris Comedy are English-language or bilingual (English + French). Each listing is clearly labelled. If you only speak English, look for shows tagged EN — there's something on every night of the week.
How do I buy tickets?
Most shows list tickets through Eventbrite. Click "Check current listing" on any show card and confirm the date on the source before you go. Some shows are free or pay-what-you-can at the door.
Can I book Robert for a private event?
Yes. Robert performs for corporate events, private parties, festivals, and birthdays — in Paris and internationally. Use the Book / Connect form to get in touch.
