Frequently Asked Questions
Everything you need to know
Are the shows in English?
Most shows on Paris Comedy are in English or in French — each listing is clearly labelled. If you only speak English, look for shows tagged EN; there are English-language shows throughout the week.
How do I buy tickets?
Most shows list tickets through Eventbrite. Click "Check current listing" on any show card and confirm the date on the source before you go. Some shows are free or pay-what-you-can at the door.
How do I promote a listing?
Sponsored placement is coming — not live yet, and nothing is charged today. See pricing for what's free right now. Listings in the directory are not ranked by source — discovery surface is neutral.
