FAQ

How do I get on the open mic?

Show up to Velvet Bar on Wednesday at 19:00 and sign up. First come, first served. 5 minutes per comic.

How much does it cost to perform?

Nothing for performers. Reserve your spot, there's a one drink minimum, and we pass the hat at the end. We're in it for the laughs.

Do I need to speak French?

Nope. Our shows are in English (some are bilingual). French is a bonus, not a requirement.

Can I book a comedian for my corporate event?

Absolutely. Email booking@pariscomedy.com with your event details and we'll match you with the right comic.

I run a comedy show in Paris. Can I get listed?

Yes! We want pariscomedy.com to be the complete guide to English comedy in Paris. Email us to get featured.
