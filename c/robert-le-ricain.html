Biography

Robert le Ricain (Robert Hoehn) is an American comedian based in Paris, on stage for fourteen years in French and English. Theatre-trained (BFA Acting, Boston University), he co-created and co-wrote “What the Fuck France” for Canal+ with Paul Taylor, and founded the French Fried Comedy Night in 2012 — the room that helped build the city's English-language scene. He also works as a professional voice actor.

Bilingual: can move between English and French live, in the room.
