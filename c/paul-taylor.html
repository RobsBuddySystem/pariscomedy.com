About

Paul Taylor is a British comedian based in France since 2009 whose comedy is the British expat in France: la bise, French administration and language mishaps. Born in Chelmsford in 1986, he performs in English and in shows that mix English and French. He calls part of his material "French bashing".

On television he made the Canal+ series "What the Fuck France?" (from September 2016), "What's Up France?" (2017) and "Stereotrip" (2018). His show "#Franglais", billed as 50% French and 50% English, ran from 2016 to 2019, playing Le Sentier des Halles and L'Européen and ending at the Casino de Paris. Time Out Paris gave it 4 out of 5. "Bisoubye x" ran from January 2023 to 6 January 2024, ending at the Zénith Paris La Villette. In 2024 he took "F*** Me I'm French!", his first English-only show, to the Edinburgh Fringe, and on 1 March 2026 he played L'Olympia in English. He co-wrote the "God Save My English" audio series with Sarah Donnelly.

He was once the subject of a question on the French quiz show "Questions pour un Champion", and says he feels 80% British and 20% French.

Little-known fact

His official site frames the current English show as the next chapter after three sold-out world tours.
