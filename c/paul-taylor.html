About

Paul Taylor is a British comedian based in France. His official site presents him as the creator of WTF France and Stereotrip, and currently spotlights F*** me I’m French!, a 100% English show about expat life in France.

Little-known fact

His official site frames the current English show as the next chapter after three sold-out world tours.
