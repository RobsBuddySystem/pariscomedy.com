Biography

Sarah Donnelly is an American comedian, screenwriter and podcast host based in Paris. She has performed at clubs, theatres and festivals across the U.S., Europe and Asia — opening for Jerry Seinfeld, touring with Gad Elmaleh, and opening for Louis CK, Roy Wood Jr., Todd Barry and Ted Alexandro. Her first hour, The Only American in Paris, is on her YouTube channel. She has a French husband and two French children, who, along with the French language, keep her humble.

Confirms in writing that she will adapt language and material for a corporate room.
