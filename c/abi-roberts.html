About

Abi Roberts is a British stand-up comedian who turned to comedy in 2011 after years as a musical and cabaret performer. She says she was the first UK comic to perform in Moscow in Russian, at the Moscow Comedy Bar and Club in February 2016.

Russia is a recurring thread. She lived in Moscow in the 1990s, and she calls her mixed-language style "Ringlish". After one Moscow gig, she has said, an audience member told her "you truly have a Russian soul".

Her shows at the Edinburgh Fringe include "Gets Her Hits Out" (2007), "Abi Roberts Experience" (2009), "Takes You Up The Aisle" (2011), "Twerk in Progress" (2014), "Downtown Abi" (2015), "Anglichanka" (2016-17) and "Fat Girl Dancing" (2017, a work in progress). In 2014 and 2015 she also ran "Musical CID", a chat show in which comedians talk about their music collections.

Little-known fact

Her official books page says Hot Banana covers a 17-hour police-cell episode after she was arrested outside the UK Covid Inquiry.
