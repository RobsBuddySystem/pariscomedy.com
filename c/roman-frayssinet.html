Roman Frayssinet

Roman Frayssinet is a French comedian who trained in Montreal after studying screenwriting at the Université de Montréal. Allociné traces his early stage work through the Couscous Comedy Show, Zoofest, École nationale de l’humour, and later French visibility through Clique and major stage shows.

Screen and stage credits

IMDb lists screen/work credits including Roman Frayssinet : Alors @ l’Olympia, Fake News and Julien Lacroix; Allociné also notes an appearance in Les Méchants.

Fun fact

Before his French breakthrough, he developed his comedy in Montreal and joined La Comédie des Trottoirs in 2015.
