About

Tania Dutel is a French stand-up comedian who performs in French about women's lives, sexuality, sexism and hair removal. Born in Villefranche-sur-Saône, she moved to Paris. Her material comes from personal anecdotes, and she avoids football and religion. Billetreduc describes her style as raw, feminist and direct.

Her first show, "Qui êtes-vous et que faites-vous dans ma chambre ?", dates from 2014. In 2017 she performed the sketch "Séance chez l'esthéticienne" at Montreux Comedy, and in January 2019 she recorded a 30-minute set for the Netflix series "Comedians of the World" ("Humoristes du monde"). She also appeared at Juste pour rire in Montréal in 2019. Her show "Les Autres" toured from 2022, with stops in Belgium, Bangkok, Singapore and Hong Kong (September 2023), and reached Australia in May 2025. It was recorded for Canal+ in 2024. Her fourth show, "Brouillage", plays at La Nouvelle Seine, in Paris's 5th arrondissement, from 18 September 2026.

She says she discovered stand-up in 2008 after seeing Mustapha El Atrassi perform.
