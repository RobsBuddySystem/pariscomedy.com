About

Fern Brady is a Scottish stand-up from Bathgate, known for the Netflix special "Autistic Bikini Queen" and the memoir "Strong Female Character". She was born in 1986.

She was joint third in So You Think You're Funny in 2011, and has taken a show to the Edinburgh Fringe repeatedly since: "People Are Idiots" (2015), "Male Comedienne" (2016), "Suffer, Fools!" (2017), "Power and Chaos" (2019), "Autistic Bikini Queen" (2022) and "I Gave You Milk to Drink" (2024). "Autistic Bikini Queen" first aired on Netflix on 22 April 2024. She appeared on "Live at the Apollo" in 2018 and in series 14 of "Taskmaster" in 2022. Her memoir (Brazen, 2023) won the Nero Book Award for non-fiction in 2024, and her debut novel, "High Energy Unpleasant", about chronic pain, has been announced.

She was misdiagnosed with OCD at 16 and diagnosed as autistic in 2021.

Little-known fact

Chortle says she first tried stand-up while writing a magazine article about pretending to be a comedian, then decided to pursue it for real.
