About

Fern Brady is a Scottish stand-up comedian, writer and podcaster. Her official site lists her as a stand-up comedian; Chortle notes she reached the So You Think You're Funny final at the Edinburgh Fringe in 2011.

Little-known fact

Chortle says she first tried stand-up while writing a magazine article about pretending to be a comedian, then decided to pursue it for real.

Screen & filmography

IMDb lists Fern Brady as an actress and writer; credits include BBC Comedy Feeds, BBC Teach: Invasion of the Book Readers and Fern Brady: Autistic Bikini Queen.

Sources & press

Chortle describes Brady as a former comedy reviewer who moved into stand-up after that assignment; her official site currently asks fans to check back for new live dates.
