🌍 Wrong Language, Right Feeling

A bilingual taste of the jokes Robert does live in Paris — built from real expat feelings, not guidebook French.

Dom insight → low-effort / high-upside bilingual hook

What does gêné mean, exactly?

Some feelings break Google Translate. Gêné is close to embarrassed, but not quite. Awkward is the panic right before the embarrassment lands. That tiny gap? That's half the Paris expat experience.

Same with ringard. It means dated — but corny is something weirder, sadder, more wedding-DJ-adjacent. That's the kind of bilingual nonsense Robert turns into live material at Velvet Bar and French Fried Comedy Night.

“My brain now thinks in both French and English at the same time. The result is neither language — just a kind of anxious fog with a baguette in it.”
“Six years in Paris and I've finally cracked the code: the secret to fitting in is having absolutely no word for how you feel.”

First Time? Here's What to Know

Everything tourists and expats ask before their first Paris comedy show.

🌍

Do I need to speak French?

Not at all. Every show on this site is in English. Some comics are bilingual — that's part of the fun. The Velvet Bar shows attract tourists and expats who want to laugh in their own language.

💶

How much does it cost?

Prices vary by room. Many shows are free to reserve on Eventbrite, while some are paid ticketed events or have a drink minimum. This page is built to show the difference before you click.

📍

Where exactly is Velvet Bar?

39 Rue de Douai, 75009 Paris — Pigalle neighbourhood. Metro: Pigalle (M2/M12), 2 minutes walk. Look for the burgundy door. Arrive 15 minutes early for the best seats.

🎙️

What's the difference between the three Wednesday shows?

19:00 Open Mic — newcomers and regulars trying new material. Raw and unpredictable in the best way. 20:30 Comedy Night — polished headliners, bigger laughs, great for first-timers. 22:00 FFCN — French Fried Comedy Night, the flagship show since 2013.

🎟️

Do I really need to reserve?

Usually yes. Even free-reservation shows can fill up, and paid shows can sell out. Use the verified booking link on each listing to check the current ticket status before you go.

👨‍👩‍👧

Is it good for groups or dates?

Both. Book a table together on Eventbrite. The room is intimate so you're close enough to feel part of the show. Great for first dates (laughing together = instant connection) and groups of 4–10 visiting Paris.
