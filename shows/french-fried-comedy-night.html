French Fried Comedy Night

Every Tuesday, 8:00 PM – 9:30 PM
MAMA Comedy Club · 26 rue des Lombards, 75004 Paris (Châtelet)
ENFREE RESERVATION · FROM €8 AT THE DOOR

French Fried Comedy Night is English-language stand-up comedy in Paris, running since 2012. It happens every Tuesday evening at MAMA Comedy Club, in the heart of the city near Châtelet. The host is Robert Hoehn.

Good to know

Is French Fried Comedy Night in English?

Yes. It is English-language stand-up comedy in Paris, and it has been running since 2012.

Do I need to book?

Reservation is free and takes a minute on Eventbrite. Each Tuesday below has its own booking link. You pay at the door, not online.

How much does it cost?

Reservation is free and you pay at the door: €8 with a soft drink, €12 with a cocktail, or €23 with a panuozzo and a drink.

Where is it?

At MAMA Comedy Club, 26 rue des Lombards, 75004 Paris, near Châtelet. MAMA is the venue; French Fried Comedy Night is the show.

What time should I arrive?

The show runs from 8:00 PM to 9:30 PM every Tuesday, so plan to be at the club shortly before 8:00 PM.
