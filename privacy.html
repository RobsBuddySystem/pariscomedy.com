Privacy

Last updated: 2026-08-02

What we collect

We collect the minimum required to run the site:

  • Information you submit via contact forms, newsletter signup, comic/venue submission forms, and booker inquiries (typically your email and the message body).
  • Anonymous analytics on page views and basic interactions (which page you visited, when, what country/region). No personal identifiers are attached.
  • Standard server logs (IP address, user-agent, timestamp) kept short-term for security and abuse prevention.

Cookies and consent

pariscomedy.com uses a consent banner on first visit. Non-essential analytics do not fire until you have made a choice — see "Analytics" below for exactly what that gates and what it doesn't. You can change your choice at any time using the button in that section.

We use browser local storage (not a tracking cookie) to remember your consent choice. We do not use third-party advertising cookies on this site, except on the homepage which may display partner offers (see "Ads" below).

Analytics

We run two separate analytics tools. They are not the same thing, and only one of them needs your permission:

  • Our own first-party beacon (page path, referrer, screen size, browser language, and a random per-tab session id — never a name, email, or IP stored against it) posts straight to our own API. It has no third-party recipient and sets no cross-site cookie. This is the "anonymous analytics" described above, it runs on every visit, and we treat it as strictly necessary to operate the site — so it is not gated behind the choice below.
  • Google Analytics (GA4, measurement ID G-1Q74JY864H) is optional and off by default. On your first visit you'll see a banner with an Accept and a Reject button — rejecting is exactly as easy as accepting, one click either way, no pre-ticked box, no follow-up nag. No request to Google of any kind is made, and no Google cookie (_ga, _gid) is set, unless and until you click Accept. Google acts as the processor; the purpose is aggregate traffic and audience measurement (visits, pages, rough location from IP, device/browser type) — we never send it a name, email, or account id. Data retention follows Google's own GA4 property configuration, not a setting we control; see Google's privacy policy for how Google itself handles it.
  • pariscomedy.com and comedyatlas.app share this one GA4 property (same project, two domains), with Google's cross-domain linking configured so moving between the two doesn't fragment a session into a false "referral" in either site's traffic reports.
  • Your choice lives in your own browser's local storage, scoped to this site only — it is not shared with comedyatlas.app or any other site, and clearing your browser storage resets it to "not asked yet" (the banner will show again on your next visit). Change your mind any time: .

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your email with venues, comics, or bookers unless you explicitly opt in via a submission form.
  • We do not run cross-site tracking pixels of social networks on every page.

Ads

A small number of pages (currently just the homepage) may display partner ads. Personalized ads only load if you have granted consent. If you decline, ads (if any) are shown in non-personalized mode and do not use cross-site tracking identifiers.

Third-party platforms

Many shows on pariscomedy.com are ticketed through external platforms (Eventbrite, BilletRéduc, Fnac, Helloasso, the venue's own page). When you follow a ticket link, you leave pariscomedy.com and that platform's privacy policy applies.

Your rights

If you submitted information through a form and want it removed, or want to know what we have on file for you, email payments@pariscomedy.com. We will respond within a reasonable time. EU/EEA/UK residents have rights under GDPR (access, deletion, portability, objection) — the same email reaches us for those requests.
