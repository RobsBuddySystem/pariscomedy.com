Editorial workflow

Small, practical, and brand-safe.

1. Pick a comedy questionChoose something audiences or comics actually ask: where to go, what to expect, or how a room differs.
2. Anchor it to live pagesEvery post should point back to relevant show, venue, history, or comedian-resource pages already on the site.
3. Keep the tone localFunny, useful, and scene-aware. No internal ops talk, no system notes, no campaign jargon.
4. End with a real next stepReserve a show, browse a venue, or submit a correction — never a dead-end article.
