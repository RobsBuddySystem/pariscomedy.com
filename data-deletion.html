Data deletion instructions

Last updated: 2026-11-08

This page explains how to remove your data from Paris Comedy (pariscomedy.com) and Comedy Atlas, including any data received through the Facebook / Instagram connection used by our app "Comedy Atlas Social".

Option 1: remove the app in Facebook or Instagram

  1. Facebook: open Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites (or "Business integrations"), find "Comedy Atlas Social", select it and choose Remove.
  2. Instagram: open Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites (or Accounts Center → Apps and websites), find "Comedy Atlas Social" and choose Remove.
  3. Removing the app revokes our access immediately. We then delete the stored access token and the data linked to your connection within 30 days.

Option 2: ask us to delete your data by email

Email contact@comedyatlas.app with the subject "Data deletion". Tell us the email address of your account and, if relevant, your Instagram or Facebook username so we can find your records. We will reply with a confirmation code.

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile and sign-in data.
  • Your Facebook / Instagram connection: account id, username, access tokens, and any insights or media metadata we stored for you.
  • Scheduled posts and drafts you created in our tools.
  • Messages and form submissions you sent us, unless we must keep a record by law (for example invoices).

Anonymous, aggregated statistics that cannot identify you are not personal data and may be kept. Security logs are rotated automatically.

How long it takes

We complete deletion within 30 days of your request or of you removing the app, and confirm by email.

Checking the status

Quote the confirmation code from our reply when you email contact@comedyatlas.app and we will tell you the current status of your request.
