About Paris Comedy

The independent home for English-language comedy in Paris.

What is this site?

ParisComedy.com is a living guide to the English-language comedy scene in Paris. We track weekly shows, venues, and the people shaping the scene — from open mic newcomers to headliners.

Our goal is simple: make it ridiculously easy to find great comedy in Paris, whether you're a local, an expat, or just in town for the weekend.

Who runs this?

Paris Comedy is run by a team of comedy lovers who believe English-language comedy in Paris deserves a proper home on the internet.

Why now?

Because the scene has exploded. What used to be one or two niche nights is now 10+ regular weekly shows across multiple venues. This city needed a central place where audiences, comics, and venues could connect.
