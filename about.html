About Paris Comedy

The Scene

Paris has a real stand-up scene — English, French, bilingual. It started in a basement in Pigalle and spread to every arrondissement. This directory is the map.

There are 34+ active shows across 27+ venues, running every night of the week. From open mics where new voices find their feet, to polished showcases that draw the best comics in the city.

Who Built This

Paris Comedy was founded by a long-running Paris comedian and audience-builder to help comics get seen and bookers find new talent.

Show listings are updated daily. We check ticket links and flag stale dates — if something's wrong, email us and we'll fix it within 24 hours.

Work With Us

Run a comedy night and want it listed? A comedian looking to get your bio in front of bookers? A booker searching for talent? Paris Comedy connects all three.
