About Paris Comedy

The Scene

Paris has a real stand-up scene — English shows and French shows. It started in a basement in Pigalle and spread to every arrondissement. This directory is the map.

Listings are added as we verify them — throughout the week, across the city. From open mics where new voices find their feet to polished showcases. Know a show that's missing? Tell us.

Who Built This

Paris Comedy was founded by a long-running Paris comedian and audience-builder to help comics get seen and bookers find new talent.

Show listings are updated daily. We check ticket links and flag stale dates — if something's wrong, email us and we'll fix it within 24 hours.

Work With Us

Run a comedy night and want it listed? A comedian looking to get your bio in front of bookers? A booker searching for talent? Paris Comedy connects all three.

We also run a booking desk: we place English/French stand-ups — Paris-based and touring Europe — into clubs and lineups, and help venues find acts. Venues or comics who want to work with us: booking@pariscomedy.com · @pariscomedy_com.
