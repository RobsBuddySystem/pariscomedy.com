About Paris Comedy

Built by the people who actually run comedy in Paris.

What This Is

ParisComedy.com is the independent guide to English-language stand-up comedy in Paris. We list every real weekly show, every active venue, and every path in — whether you want to watch, perform, or get your night on the map.

We're not a ticket agency and we're not a blog. We're the platform the Paris comedy scene was missing: one place that actually stays current, covers all the shows, and gives audiences a reason to show up.

Who Built It

🍟

Robert Hoehn — Founder

Comedian · French Fried Comedy Night · Since 2013

American bilingual comedian who launched French Fried Comedy Night in 2013 at Paname Art Café — now the longest-running English-language stand-up show in Paris. He gave Paul Taylor his first regular stage. He produced and directed La Bise, the viral bilingual video that put the Paris English comedy scene on the map. He's been running the Wednesday night triple-header at Velvet Bar ever since. This site is an extension of that work.
