Tickets & Affiliate Disclosure

Paris Comedy is a directory of comedy shows in Paris. We list shows, venues, and comedians. We do not sell tickets directly.

Where tickets are actually sold

When you click a "Get tickets / source listing" link on a Paris Comedy show page, you are taken to a third-party ticket platform run by someone else. The most common third parties are:

  • Eventbrite
  • FNAC / France Billet
  • BilletReduc
  • Fever
  • Ticketmaster France
  • See Tickets
  • Weezevent
  • Billetweb
  • Yurplan
  • HelloAsso
  • Shotgun
  • Dice
  • Direct venue websites, Instagram, or Facebook event pages

Each of these platforms has its own pricing, terms of sale, refund policy, and customer service. Paris Comedy is not a party to your ticket purchase.

Affiliate relationships

If Paris Comedy has an affiliate or referral arrangement with a ticket platform, a venue, or a show, that relationship will be disclosed here as soon as it goes live. As of 2026-05-29, Paris Comedy has no active paid-affiliate arrangements. All third-party ticket links currently point directly to the show's published source URL without affiliate tags. We may add affiliate tags in the future for clearly disclosed platforms — that will be listed in this section.

Sponsored show promotion

Some shows or venues may pay Paris Comedy for a featured / highlighted slot. When this happens, that show will be visibly marked as "Promoted" or "Featured", and the highlighted_until date will be set in our public data. Promotion does not affect Paris Comedy's listings for other shows in the same time slot.

Freshness and accuracy

Each show on Paris Comedy carries a Last checked date and a verification status. You can see these on every show page. Times, lineups, and prices can change without notice — always confirm on the source link before purchase. If a show is older than 24 hours since last verification, the source platform is the authoritative reference.

Compliance

Paris Comedy does not collect payment details. We do not store payment cards. We do not initiate ticket purchases on your behalf. For terms governing your use of this directory, see the Terms of Use; for what we collect about visitors, see the Privacy Policy.
